Veggie & Hummus Sandwich

Recipe Creator:
Abdullah Ali

Details

Category: Iftar

A quick, wholesome sandwich full of crunchy veggies and creamy hummus — perfect for a light iftar. Loaded with fiber, vitamins, and plant-based protein from chickpeas.

Ingredients

  • Whole-grain bread — 2 slices
  • Hummus — 3 tbsp
  • Cucumber slices — ¼ cup
  • Tomato slices — ¼ cup
  • Shredded carrot — ¼ cup
  • Lettuce or mixed greens — Handful
  • Salt & black pepper — Pinch

Instructions

  1. Spread hummus evenly on both bread slices.
  2. Layer lettuce, cucumber, tomato, and carrot.
  3. Season lightly with salt and black pepper.
  4. Close the sandwich, cut in half, and serve.
