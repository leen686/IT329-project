Veggie & Hummus Sandwich
Category: Iftar
  • Bread — 2 slices (whole-grain)
  • Hummus — 3 tbsp
  • Cucumber — ¼ cup, sliced
  • Tomato — ¼ cup, sliced
  • Carrot — ¼ cup, shredded
  • Lettuce or mixed greens — handfu
  • Salt — 1 pinch
  • Black pepper — 1 pinch
  1. Spread hummus evenly on both bread slices.
  2. Layer lettuce, cucumber, tomato, and carrot.
  3. Season lightly with salt and black pepper.
  4. Close the sandwich, cut in half, and serve.
