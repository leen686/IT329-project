Wild Rice Mushroom Soup

Recipe Creator:
Razan Faisal

Details

Category: Iftar

A cozy, satisfying wild rice mushroom soup — rich in fiber and earthy flavor from mushrooms, hearty enough for iftar to refill energy after fasting.

Ingredients

  • Olive oil — 1 tbsp
  • Onion, chopped — 1
  • Mushrooms, sliced — 250 g
  • Wild rice — 1 cup
  • Vegetable broth — 5–6 cups
  • Dried thyme — ½ tsp
  • Salt & black pepper — To taste
  • Milk or cooking cream (optional) — ¼ cup

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil and sauté onion until soft.
  2. Add mushrooms and cook until tender.
  3. Stir in rice, broth, thyme, salt, and pepper.
  4. Simmer 35–40 minutes until rice is cooked.
  5. Add milk or cream if desired, stir, and serve warm.
