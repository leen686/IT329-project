Energy Balls

Recipe Creator:
Sami Ahmed

Details

Category: Snacks

Simple no-bake energy balls made with oats, nut butter, and honey — great for a healthy snack or to boost energy after suhoor.

Ingredients

  • Rolled oats — 1½ cups
  • Peanut butter — ¾ cup
  • Honey or date syrup — ¼ cup
  • Ground flaxseed — ¼ cup
  • Shredded coconut — ¼ cup
  • Vanilla extract — 1 tsp

Instructions

  1. Mix oats, peanut butter, honey, flaxseed, and vanilla in a bowl.
  2. Scoop small portions and roll into balls.
  3. Roll each ball in shredded coconut.
  4. Chill in the fridge for 1 hour before serving.
