Creamy Lemon Pasta with Shrimp

Recipe Creator:
Lina Osama

Details

Category: Suhoor

A refreshing lemon shrimp pasta that’s hearty enough for suhoor yet bright and healthy with olive oil, fresh lemon, and shrimp protein.

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti — 8 oz
  • Shrimp, peeled & deveined — 400–500 g
  • Olive oil — 2 tbsp
  • Garlic cloves, minced — 3 cloves
  • Lemon zest & juice — 1 lemon
  • Cooking cream — ¼ cup
  • Salt & black pepper — To taste
  • Fresh parsley or herbs — For garnish

Instructions

  1. Cook pasta until al dente; drain.
  2. Heat olive oil and sauté garlic briefly.
  3. Add shrimp, salt, and pepper; cook until pink.
  4. Stir in cream, lemon juice, and zest.
  5. Add pasta, toss gently, garnish, and serve.
